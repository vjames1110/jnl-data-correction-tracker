JNL Project Monitor

Single-sheet monitoring: structures · linear works · action items
How to use: set the meeting date at the top before a review. Every date you revise is stamped with that meeting date — the earlier date stays visible with a strike and the new date appears beside it with your comment. Data saves automatically in this browser; use Export to keep a file copy / share with site, and Import to load it elsewhere.

Step 1 · Define scope (grey band)

Enter the chainage stretches where this activity is required (as many patches as needed — gaps are not in scope). For cum / nos items also enter the quantity for that stretch; for running-metre items the length is taken from the chainage.

Step 2 · Daily progress entry

Enter each day's work by chainage. Mark a stretch Ongoing when work starts there (yellow); later enter only the portion Completed (green) — it overlays the yellow and the balance stays ongoing.
